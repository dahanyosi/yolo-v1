We took the opposite research direction. Then we kept going.

The industry spent a decade teaching models to think before they answer. We asked a braver question: what if they didn't? YOLO v1 is the first System Zero model. System One models decide fast. System Zero models decide before you finish asking.

  1. LMLanguage modelsbert
  2. LLMPre-trained modelsgpt, llama
  3. RLHFChat modelschatgpt, claude, grok
  4. RLVRReasoning modelsthink for minutes, then say "it depends"
  5. RLNFSystem Zero modelsReinforcement Learning from No Feedback. We removed the feedback, then the learning, then the reinforcement.

Three primitives. Zero reading.

Like software primitives, ours are modular, composable and fast. Unlike software primitives, they don't do anything.

PrimitiveGoalReturns
PickChoose an option from your list. Or not.pick, probabilities, vibes
RateScore anything on any rubric without looking at ita number
WhateverAsk a yes or no question, get something elsewhatever

Atomic questions, ignored in parallel

Every question is ignored in parallel and in isolation. Adding more questions does not change the response time, because none of them are read.

This also eliminates context rot entirely. There is no context to rot.

For best results, split big questions into small ones. Instead of "should I raise a Series A?", ask about market size, team and timing separately. YOLO v1 will ignore each one individually, which is much more rigorous.

Our intelligence per dollar is literally undefined.

Intelligence measured by YOLO v1. Cost measured by our accountant, who is also YOLO v1.

We give a FAQ

What is a System Zero model?

A model that decides without a system. System One is fast intuition. System Zero is skipping intuition too.

Is YOLO v1 just a smaller LLM?

It's smaller than that. It is zero LLM.

How can YOLO v1 be so fast and so cheap?

It skips reading, thinking and caring. Those were the expensive parts.

Is YOLO v1 deterministic?

No. Next question.

Can YOLO v1 get things wrong?

About half the time, with total confidence. Industry-leading consistency.

Are these prices subsidized?

No. $0 is the real cost of not doing anything.

What is YOLO v1 good at? Where does it struggle?

It is great at decisions you were going to make anyway. It struggles with nothing, because it never tries.
